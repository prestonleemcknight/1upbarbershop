What this site collects

This website has no contact form, no account sign-up and no newsletter box, so it does not collect your name, email or phone number directly. It stores no cookies of its own and runs no advertising or analytics trackers.

Where you leave this site

Some buttons take you to services run by other companies. Once you are there, that company’s own privacy policy applies, not ours:

  • Our online booking provider, when you book an appointment.
  • Google Maps, when you tap Get Directions or open our reviews.
  • Instagram, when you open our profile.

Booking and appointment details

When you book, the name, phone number and appointment details you enter are held by our booking provider and used to run your appointment — confirming it, reminding you and getting you in the chair. We do not sell that information.
