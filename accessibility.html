Accessibility Statement

Last updated: [ADD DATE BEFORE PUBLISHING]

We want everyone to be able to find our hours, see what we charge and book a cut — on any device, with any assistive technology.

What we have built in

  • Semantic headings and landmarks, so screen readers can move through the page by section.
  • A skip link to jump straight past the navigation to the main content.
  • Visible keyboard focus on every link, button and control.
  • Text and interface colors checked against WCAG 2.1 AA contrast ratios.
  • Tap targets at least 44 × 44 pixels.
  • A keyboard-operable menu and photo gallery, both closable with Escape.
  • Descriptive alternative text on images, and decorative graphics hidden from screen readers.
  • Animation that stops entirely when your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Layout that reflows to 320 pixels wide without horizontal scrolling.

Our target

We aim to meet WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Parts of this site rely on services run by other companies — our booking provider and Google Maps — and we do not control their accessibility.
